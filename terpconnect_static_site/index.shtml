Clay Court Notes

Tennis is a sport of geometry and patience. This page is a short field guide to the surface I like most: clay. Longer rallies, heavier balls, and more time to think between shots.

Surface comparison

Surface Typical bounce What it rewards
Clay High and slow Footwork, topspin, endurance
Hard Medium and true Balanced offense and defense
Grass Low and skiddy Serve, slice, first-strike tennis
Indoor hard Fast and consistent Clean timing and return of serve
